# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and professional. Welcome different viewpoints, focus on the work rather than the person, and assume good faith while addressing disagreements directly and calmly.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or close contributions that violate this code.
